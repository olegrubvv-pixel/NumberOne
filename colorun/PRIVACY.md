# ColoRun Privacy Policy

**Version 0.1**  
**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

## Overview

ColoRun is an offline arcade game. This version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or gameplay data with the developer or third parties.

## Data stored on your device

Your best score, run count, sound preference, and vibration preference are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted off your device. You can remove it by using the in-game Reset progress option, by clearing the app's local data, or by uninstalling the app.

## Accounts, advertising, and analytics

This version of ColoRun does not provide user accounts and does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, cloud synchronization, or third-party tracking.

## Device permissions and features

The game does not require access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, phone, or files. If vibration is enabled, the device vibration feature is used only to provide local gameplay feedback.

## Secure data handling

ColoRun version 0.1 does not transmit personal or gameplay data to a developer-controlled server. Local game progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage on the device and are handled by the device operating system and app storage environment.

## Children's privacy

ColoRun does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from other users.

## Data retention and deletion

Because this version does not send user data to a server, the developer does not retain personal user data. Locally stored game progress and settings remain on the device until the user resets progress, clears app data, or uninstalls the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions or privacy-related inquiries, users can contact the developer using the developer support contact shown on the ColoRun listing in Google Play.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online services, advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud features, or other data processing, this privacy policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.

---

This privacy policy applies to **ColoRun version 0.1**.
